Suggest an opportunity

Know a volunteering opportunity in Dubai that students could join? Or spotted a listing that is out of date? Send an email and it will be checked against the organisation’s own website before anything is added or changed.

Email a suggestion

The button opens your own email app, addressed to . This site has no forms and collects nothing.

What helps most

  • The organisation’s name and a link to its own volunteering page.
  • What volunteers actually do.
  • The minimum age, if you know it.
  • Where it is in Dubai, or whether it can be done from home.

What cannot be listed

  • Fundraising, donation appeals or collection drives of any kind.
  • Anything without a real organisation and a working link.
  • Private contact details that the organisation has not published itself.
