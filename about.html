About OpenYad

“Yad” is the Arabic word for hand, so OpenYad means open hand. The site was made by a student in Dubai because finding volunteering as a teenager is hard: the big platforms are built for adults, many listings are 18+, and it is rarely clear how much time something takes or how to get there.

What this site is

OpenYad is a directory of volunteering opportunities in Dubai, aimed mainly at students aged 13 to 18, including Duke of Edinburgh’s International Award participants. It helps you find something that fits your interests, your free time and where you live.

What this site is not

  • It does not register volunteers. Every listing links to the organisation’s own sign-up page or public contact details. You sign up with them, not here.
  • It does not take donations. There are no donate buttons, fundraising pages or collection drives, and none will be listed.
  • It is not run by, or connected to, any organisation listed.

Please confirm details with the organiser

Each listing was taken from the organisation’s own website and shows the date it was last checked. Things change, so always confirm the minimum age, times, place and any parent consent rules with the organiser before you go. Where a detail could not be confirmed, the listing says “Check with organiser” instead of guessing.

Nearest Metro stations are marked “approximate” because they are worked out from a map, not provided by the organisation.

If you are under 18

  • Ask a parent or guardian before you sign up for anything or share your details with an organisation.
  • Many organisations need a parent’s consent, or an adult to come with you.
  • For Duke of Edinburgh or school service hours, check with your Award leader or teacher that the activity counts before you start.

Your privacy

  • No accounts, no sign-up forms, and no names or emails collected.
  • No tracking, analytics or advertising scripts.
  • Your saved list, the age and area you pick, and your “My Hand” points are stored only in your own browser. They never leave your device.
  • Distance is worked out in your browser from the area you choose. The site never asks for your GPS location.
