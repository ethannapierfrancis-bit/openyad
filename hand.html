    How points work

    • 10 points for every hour you volunteer.
    • +15 the first time you try a new cause.
    • +5 when you go with friends.
    • +10 each week you keep a weekly streak going.
    • Every 20 points fills one shape. Fill all 40 to reach Gold Yad.

    This is an honesty system, just for you. Your log is saved only in this browser and is never sent anywhere. A shared link shows only your points and colours, with no name. It is not an official record, so keep your Duke of Edinburgh or school log separately.
